# T-0004 Spec

## Task metadata
- Task ID: T-0004
- Title: Add obstacles and enemies
- Lane: feature-lane
- Executor: codex (ChatGPT)
- Depends on: T-0002 (player movement), T-0003 (basic collision)

## Problem statement
The game currently supports a single player entity with basic movement and gravity. To create challenging gameplay, we need:
1. Static obstacles (spike tiles) that kill the player on contact
2. Destructible platforms (crumbling platforms) that break after brief contact
3. Moving platforms that translate horizontally or vertically
4. Enemy NPCs that patrol and apply game-over on side collision, but can be defeated by jumping on them

## Source of truth
- CONFIG: CANVAS_WIDTH:800, CANVAS_HEIGHT:400, TILE_SIZE:16, GRAVITY:0.6
- Single-file implementation: index.html
- Player collision system from T-0003
- Existing gameState structure

## Desired behavior

### Spike tiles
- Static, non-animated obstacles
- Positioned on the tilemap
- Instant kill on any contact (top, bottom, left, right)
- Visual: distinct color/sprite to indicate danger
- No physics interaction (player stops at collision boundary)

### Crumbling platforms
- Platform tiles that appear solid initially
- Start breaking after player stands on them for ~0.5 seconds
- Animation: visual flicker/crack effect during break phase (~0.3 seconds)
- Once broken, platform becomes non-solid for ~2 seconds, then respawns
- Player falls through during break phase if still on platform

### Moving platforms
- Horizontal movers: patrol left-right between two x-positions, move at 2 tiles/sec
- Vertical movers: patrol up-down between two y-positions, move at 1 tile/sec
- Platform carries the player (relative motion) while player stands on it
- Respawn at start position if they fall off-screen
- Configurable via tilemap metadata or inline objects

### Enemy NPCs
- Type 1: Walker - 2x2 tile sprite, patrols left-right on platforms, moves at 1.5 tiles/sec
- Type 2: Flyer - 2x2 tile sprite, patrols in a horizontal range in mid-air, moves at 2 tiles/sec
- Simple 2-frame walk animation (alternating legs), no idle state
- Patrol bounds: turn around at platform edges or at configurable waypoints
- Collision from sides/bottom: player takes damage (game over or health loss)
- Collision from top (jumping on head): enemy dies, shows brief death animation, player bounces with +0.3 boost to velocity.y

### Collision detection
- Extend existing collision system (from T-0003) to handle new entities
- Determine collision direction: top, bottom, left, right
- Separate entity responses:
  - Spike: instant game over
  - Crumbling: start break timer
  - Moving platform: apply relative velocity to player
  - Enemy top: kill enemy, bounce player
  - Enemy side: game over

## Constraints
- Single-file HTML implementation (no bundler)
- Max 8 sprites per screen concurrently (performance target)
- Collision detection must run at 60 FPS without lag
- Animations must be frame-based, not time-based (frame % spritesheet size)
- All sprites are 16x16 or 32x32 (multiples of TILE_SIZE)
- No external sprite libraries; use canvas drawImage with sprite sheet
- Enemies and platforms must be storable in gameState for persistence across frames
- Tilemap definition: simple 2D array or grid encoding (e.g., spike = 2, crumble = 3, patrol-h = 4, patrol-v = 5)

## Acceptance criteria
1. Spike tiles kill player on contact from any direction
2. Crumbling platform respects timing: solid for 0.5s, cracks for 0.3s, broken for 2s, then respawns
3. Moving platforms transport player at correct relative velocity
4. Walker enemy patrols horizontally, dies when jumped on, bounces player upward
5. Flyer enemy patrols horizontally in mid-air, dies when jumped on, bounces player upward
6. Jumping on enemy kills it and prevents simultaneous player death
7. Side collision with enemy causes game over
8. Enemies animate at 10 FPS (5 frames * 2-frame walk cycle = 10 FPS with frame updates every 6 game ticks)
9. Collision detection correctly identifies top vs. side collisions for enemy interactions
10. New entity types are stored in gameState.enemies[] and gameState.platforms[] for persistence

## Risks
1. **Edge case: rapid enemy respawning** - If player dies to enemy and respawns in same location, enemy may already be there. Mitigation: Enemies don't spawn until player is 4+ tiles away.
2. **Collision direction ambiguity** - Diagonal collisions could be interpreted as top or side. Mitigation: Use previous frame velocity to disambiguate (if moving downward, it's a top collision).
3. **Platform carry velocity bleed** - If platform moves fast, player velocity.y might not update correctly. Mitigation: Apply platform velocity as offset, not replacement.
4. **Performance with many enemies** - Need to track enemy state (walking frame, patrol direction, break timer, death state). Mitigation: Limit to 8 concurrent entities per screen.
5. **Tilemap encoding ambiguity** - If tilemap is visually ambiguous, spikes could be mistaken for normal tiles. Mitigation: Use distinct color palette and document in tilemap comments.

## Open questions
1. Should moving platforms wrap at screen edges, or just turn around? (Assumption: turn around)
2. What is the damage model? Does enemy contact deal damage or instant kill? (Assumption: instant game over for now, XP system in T-0006 may change this)
3. Should crumbling platforms respawn at original position or fall off-screen? (Assumption: respawn at original)
4. Can players stand on flying enemies? (Assumption: yes, same as walker)
5. Should enemy patrol direction reverse when hitting a wall, or is there a waypoint system? (Assumption: reverse at platform edges)

# T-0004 Implementation Brief

## Goal
Add static obstacles (spikes), destructible platforms (crumbling), moving platforms, and enemy NPCs (walkers, flyers) to create challenging platformer gameplay. Players can defeat enemies by jumping on them but take damage from side contact.

## Scope
1. **Spike tiles** - Instant kill on any contact; static; no animation
2. **Crumbling platforms** - Solid for 0.5s contact, then crack animation (0.3s), then non-solid for 2s, auto-respawn
3. **Moving platforms** - Horizontal (2 tiles/sec) and vertical (1 tile/sec) patrol; carry player as moving parent
4. **Walker enemies** - 2x2 tile, patrol left-right at 1.5 tiles/sec, 2-frame walk animation
5. **Flyer enemies** - 2x2 tile, patrol horizontally in mid-air at 2 tiles/sec, 2-frame walk animation
6. **Collision system** - Extend T-0003 AABB collision to identify direction (top, bottom, left, right) for different entity responses
7. **Tilemap encoding** - Support spike (2), crumble (3), patrol-h (4), patrol-v (5) tile IDs; patrol-h and patrol-v objects store bounds and speed metadata

## Constraints
- Single-file HTML (index.html)
- Max 8 concurrent dynamic entities (enemies + moving platforms, excluding static spikes/crumbles)
- 60 FPS game loop; collision runs every frame
- Animations frame-based, not time-based (use `gameState.frameCounter % spritesheetFrames`)
- Sprites are 16x16 or 32x32 (multiples of TILE_SIZE:16)
- No external libraries; canvas drawImage with sprite sheet
- Tilemap grid size: 50x25 tiles (800x400px ÷ 16px/tile)

## File targets
- **index.html** - Add entity types (Spike, Crumble, MovingPlatform, Enemy), collision detection handlers, animation frame counters, entity update/draw functions

## Tests required
1. Spike kills player on any direction contact
2. Crumbling platform: solid 0.5s, cracks 0.3s, broken 2s, respawns automatically
3. Moving platform horizontal: transports player with correct relative velocity
4. Moving platform vertical: transports player with correct relative velocity
5. Walker enemy: patrols left-right, dies on top collision, bounces player (velocity.y = -5.0)
6. Flyer enemy: patrols in mid-air, dies on top collision, bounces player
7. Enemy side collision: game over
8. Top vs. side collision disambiguation: velocity.y < 0 → top; else → side
9. Platform carry velocity: player input velocity.x is added to platform velocity.x
10. Multiple enemies on same platform: independent patrol, no mutual collisions

## Chosen minimal policy
- **Collision direction**: Use velocity.y sign: if < 0 (downward velocity), treat as top collision; else treat as side. No corner-case averaging.
- **Crumbling platform timer**: Track per-tile standTime in platform object; reset to 0 on new landing (not re-landing during break).
- **Enemy bounce**: Set velocity.y = -5.0 (concrete value) for upward bounce.
- **Patrol bounds**: Walkers reverse at platform edge (rightmost solid platform tile); flyers reverse at configurable waypoint bounds (or screen edge if none specified).
- **Respawn behavior**: Moving platforms and enemies respawn at spawn position if center moves off-screen (x < -32 or x > CANVAS_WIDTH + 32).
- **Sprite animation**: All enemies use shared 2-frame walk cycle; update frame on `gameState.frameCounter % 6 == 0` (10 FPS from 60 FPS game loop).
- **Performance**: Enemies and platforms use simple AABB collision; limit total dynamic entities to 8 per level (enforced at level design time).

## Risks
1. **Collision detection regression** - Adding 5+ entity types to collision system may slow frame rate. Mitigation: profile before/after; optimize AABB checks if FPS drops.
2. **Edge case: multiple collisions same frame** - Player could collide with spike and enemy simultaneously. Mitigation: process collisions in order (spike first, fatal). Once player is dead, skip remaining collision checks.
3. **Tilemap encoding collision** - If tilemap uses numeric IDs, must carefully avoid conflicts with existing IDs. Mitigation: ensure IDs (2, 3, 4, 5) don't collide with any prior encoding.
4. **Moving platform velocity bleed** - If platform moves fast (> 2 tiles/sec), player velocity.y might not update. Mitigation: apply platform velocity as offset, not replacement; test with max speed.
5. **Enemy respawn collision** - Enemy respawns at spawn position while player is still alive nearby. Mitigation: disable collision during first 0.5s of respawn (invulnerable phase) or respawn only when player is 4+ tiles away.

## Explicit non-goals
- No advanced AI (enemies follow fixed patrol only, no targeting or pathfinding)
- No particle effects for deaths/bounces (reserved for T-0007)
- No sound effects (reserved for T-0007)
- No difficulty levels or enemy variety scaling (fixed walker and flyer types)
- No boss encounters
- No dynamic platform creation/destruction (all platforms spawned at level start)
- No enemy knockback (enemy dies, no momentum transfer to player except upward bounce)
- No multi-hit enemies (all enemies die in one jump)
