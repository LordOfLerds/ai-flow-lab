Corrected core business model

The central business entity is a PurchaseCase, not an auction and not a generic “purchase amount excluding tax.” A PurchaseCase is the operational case derived from one imported accounting/document block from a K-list workbook and then enriched through review, Bidware lookup, revenue decisions, lot follow-up, and possibly Dropbox fallback.

Main entities
PurchaseCase

Represents one operational case derived from K-list import logic in src/klist/import_pipeline/importer.py and stored through the repository layer.

Business meaning:

One economic case from one accounting block, usually anchored by a debtor/customer row.

It may later link to zero, one, or many auctions.

It may finish through auction revenue, Dropbox manual remaining value, or stay blocked.

Important fields in business terms:

purchase_name_raw: visible real-world case name from the import, often containing insolvency markers like IV, MV, law firms, or masseverwalter names.

tax_type: not a VAT percentage enum like 19% or 7%. In this project the meaningful buckets are business paths such as normal, differential, mixed, and similar domain-specific states.

initial_value_net, initial_value_diff: starting value buckets.

current_value_net, current_value_diff: effective current values after decisions.

status: workflow state, not just UI decoration.

excluded, excluded_reason: explicitly removed from operational handling.

dropbox_checked, dropbox_manual_rest_value: fallback state and final remaining value.

Represents one uploaded K-list import event.

Business meaning:

A traceable import run.
